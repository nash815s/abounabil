# Design Brief — "الحاج أبو نبيل" (Abu Nabil) Website

## Project context
A satirical LinkedIn carousel series about workplace culture, soon expanding to Instagram, built around a fictional boss archetype called "الحاج أبو نبيل" (Abu Nabil). Each episode opens with a scene or moment, then shifts into a deeper reading after a "جديا" (seriously) break. The site needed is a small gathering point: explains the premise, shows a preview of the episodes, points visitors to Instagram and LinkedIn, and includes a simple form for readers to submit their own stories.

This may grow into something bigger later (a full episode archive, contributor community), but this first version should stay small and restrained — don't design for anything beyond what's listed below.

**Site content language: Arabic, full RTL layout.** Everything else — this brief, your working process, code, comments, class names, file names — should be in English.

## Visual identity (locked from the existing carousels — follow exactly)
- Primary background: `#f4e8d0` (warm cream)
- Primary text/accent colour: `#2f5e5a` (deep teal)
- Darker shade for contrast: `#1f403d`
- Main ink/body text colour: `#3b2f22` (warm dark brown)
- Secondary accent: `#a85a3d` (faded brick) — use sparingly, not a primary colour
- Overall feel: picture-book page, soft watercolour tones, nothing sharp, glossy, or "startup-looking." Avoid hard gradients, crisp drop shadows, or a tech-product aesthetic.
- Typography: headings in a typeface with a classical/calligraphic character (e.g. Aref Ruqaa), body text in something calmer and more readable (e.g. Markazi Text). Avoid cold geometric modern sans-serifs.
- Framing: the Canva carousel slides use a slightly uneven double border around each slide — a hand-drawn feel, not sharp rectangles. Carry that sensibility into site elements (cards, buttons) where it fits naturally.

## The character (context only — do not redesign or regenerate)
Early sixties, silver-grey hair, thick groomed moustache, small thin wire-frame glasses, pale blue long-sleeve shirt, seated at a cluttered old wooden desk with stamps and folders. Illustration style: loose expressive linework, flat watercolour washes, warm picture-book feel — no heavy ink outlines, no dense shading.

**Actual illustrations of the character already exist separately and should not be generated by Claude Design.** Leave clearly marked placeholder slots at a 4:5 aspect ratio for episode cover images, to be swapped in later with the real artwork from Midjourney/Canva.

## Site structure (full scrolling site, not a single framed page)
1. **Hero** — title, short tagline, space for a main cover image (placeholder, roughly 4:5 or slightly wider)
2. **Backstory** — one or two paragraphs on how the series started (placeholder text only, will be replaced with final copy)
3. **Episode preview** — a row or grid of 3–4 cards, each a placeholder episode cover image + episode title, linking out to the original post
4. **Follow links** — clear buttons for Instagram and LinkedIn
5. **Share your story** — simple form: a text area for the story, an optional email field, a submit button
6. **Footer** — simple, minimal

## Tone and voice
Any explanatory copy on the site (outside the episodes themselves) should be plain and unhurried — no marketing register, no stock phrases or overused turns of phrase. No need for forced enthusiasm or big promises; this is a small doorway, not a product being sold.

## Technical notes
- Output needs to be exportable HTML/CSS that merges cleanly into an existing GitHub Pages repository (currently holding index.html and style.css, same palette as above).
- Site content is Arabic only, full RTL.
- Responsive/mobile-first matters — a large share of visitors will arrive from Instagram or LinkedIn on a phone.
